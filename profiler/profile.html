0. Read this first: not a terms-of-service

This isn't a "terms of service" agreement and you do NOT want to treat it like one. We've made it really simple and fast; for your best safety in the world of AI, you need to be sure you're really reading it, and not just scrolling to the bottom and clicking OK.

This isn't a user agreement. This is your self-directed decisions about how AI is allowed to see you, know you, use your information, and interact with you, and what it's allowed to do with any of the information it gathers in the process.

While it's always a good idea to occasionally audit your profile, which is really easy to do, it's also intended to be a "set it and forget it" system to the greatest extent possible without cutting corners on user safety.

Flags on your end mean nothing unless the other end reads them. A green setting here is a claim, not a shield. 1. The honest default

When you use an AI, your words leave your machine and land on somebody else's computer. That computer can keep them, train on them, hand them over, get breached.

Before you tell a cloud provider anything: this isn't the place for your most intimate thoughts. Frontier cloud is public-facing. Leaks are always possible. We'd rather tell you now than have you discover it later. Your settings here sit inside two harder constraints that always win: the law and their rules. Everything else is yours to draw.

Five tiers, strictest last. Unknown flags mean most-private. That is the safe default, not a bug.

2. Build your vault: scenarios, not checkboxes

You're not filling out a privacy form. You're designing your data vault: which rooms exist, who gets keys. Each class arrives as a scenario. You choose what happens next.

What the tiers mean — pick per row:
UF — Use Freely: Improve the service, inform advertising, the works.
SO — Service Only: Use it to make things better; keep it out of advertising.
CO — Conversation only: Help me right now, then forget it. This conversation only.
NK — No Keep: Seen to route, not retained. Transient processing only — the discard path is auditable.
NT — No Transmission: Never leaves your device. Local inference only. Strictest — unknown defaults here.
